The HR Prompt Formula

Strong prompts give the model a role, one task, hiring context, output shape, and guardrails.

FieldWhat to include
RoleWho the AI acts as (e.g., recruitment analyst, interview specialist)
TaskOne deliverable (diagnose funnel, draft interview plan, phrase offer)
ContextRole, location (US), stage, company size — no candidate PII
FormatBullets, table, max length, tone
ConstraintsEEO-aware, US formats ($, MM/DD/YYYY), what to avoid

Prompt 1 — Diagnose the funnel~5–10 min

You are a recruitment analyst. Identify where recruitment is stuck and what to change, using the numbers.

Company/location: [company, e.g., New York, NY]
Role location: [e.g., Remote – US, Hybrid – New York, NY, or On-site – Austin, TX]
Role: [job title]   State: [two-letter, e.g., NY]   Zip Code: [optional]

We have:
- Candidates: [ ]   Interviews: [ ]   Offers: [ ]   Accepted: [ ]

Explain: 1) Where are we most stuck? 2) Why? 3) What can we change this week?
Sample AI output — AE role, Austin, TX

Most stuck: interview-to-offer (2 offers from 14 interviews = 14%).

Why: screening passes candidates who miss the discovery competency you score in panel.

This week: tighten the screen with a 3-question discovery probe; calibrate panel on one shared rubric anchor.
