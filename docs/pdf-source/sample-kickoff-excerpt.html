ExcerptKickoff

15-minute hiring kickoff worksheet

Print this page. Run it before you open ChatGPT. Hiring manager and HR fill it together.

Role title & level — reporting line, headcount this seat replaces.
3 must-have competencies — not 15 nice-to-haves.
Salary band approved internally — write the band, not "TBD".
Interview panel named — who owns which competency.
Target start date (MM/DD/YYYY) and funnel goal (applicants → offers).
Knockout criteria — documented (e.g., must work US business hours).
Where we are stuck today — write the funnel counts (applicants, interviews, offers).

Signatures

Date (MM/DD/YYYY):

After kickoff: define who fits, then rewrite the ad, then source. Do not skip the profile — it prevents generic job ads.
