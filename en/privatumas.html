Privacy policy

US Hiring Prompt Library – a minimal prompt workflow for HR teams. Briefly about your data.

Do we collect your data?

No. We do not collect any personal data at this time. No forms, email collection or server submission.

What happens on your device?

Only the browser localStorage (browser local storage): we save which prompts you marked as “Marked as done”. Data stays only on your device.

If we add a form later

If we enable a contact form or similar, this policy will be updated – we will clearly state what we collect and how we use it.
