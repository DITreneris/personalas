What data we process

Free tool: We do not collect personal data on our servers — no account, no email signup, no analytics cookies.

Paid PDFs: Stripe processes your payment and shares your billing email and payment metadata with us so we can fulfill your order (download link by email).

What stays on your device

Only browser localStorage: which prompts you marked as done. That data never leaves your device unless you clear site data.

Sub-processors (paid PDFs)

Stripe (payments), Resend (transactional email), and Upstash Redis (fulfillment state and signed download tokens). We retain fulfillment records for up to 90 days. See Terms for license and refunds.

We load typography from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com. Those providers may receive your IP address and browser metadata. We do not run advertising or social tracking pixels on this site today.
